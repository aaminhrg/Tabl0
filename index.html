How it works

From QR code to kitchen ticket

No new hardware, no separate app for your staff to learn. Print a code, put it on the table, done.

1

Build your menu once

Categories, items, prices in EGP, and variants — size, milk type, cup type — set up in minutes with reusable templates, not typed from scratch every time.

2

Print a QR code per table

Generated automatically the moment you add a table. No design work, no third-party QR tool.

3

Customer scans, orders, pays

Opens instantly in their browser or your native app — no download required to place a first order.

4

Your kitchen sees it immediately

Live order queue updates in real time, with sound alerts so nothing gets missed during a rush.
